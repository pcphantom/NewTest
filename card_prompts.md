# Card Art Generation Prompts

These prompts create illustration assets only.

The runtime card frame supplies the card name, type, symbols, values, rules text and flavor text. Generated artwork must contain none of those elements.

## Universal art specification

Use this specification at the beginning of every card prompt:

> Create foreground illustration art for a dark-comedy retro fantasy card battler. Use deliberate late-1990s PC RPG pixel-art influence with chunky readable forms, crisp hard edges, limited color ramps, strong silhouettes and dramatic tabletop-card composition. Compose for a 4:3 landscape art window with the important subject centered inside the safe middle 80% of the canvas. No card frame. No border. No title. No letters. No numbers. No UI. No icons representing gameplay values. No sword, shield, heart, draw or lightning gameplay symbols unless the depicted object is literally part of the scene. No watermark. The image must work as pure illustration independent of card rules.

Recommended source size: 1024 x 768 PNG.

Recommended runtime path:

`assets/card-art/<card_definition_id>.png`

## Character art direction

### Grandpa the Grayscale

Visual identity:

- elderly fantasy wizard
- long gray beard
- layered gray robe
- chunky old CRT and monochrome-computer motifs
- grayscale base palette
- phosphor green and dim amber accents
- dry, irritated expression
- ancient technology treated as magical relics

### Malric the Meat Shield

Visual identity:

- broad armored tank
- oversized battered shield
- practical plate and leather
- iron gray, dark leather brown, muted red and shield blue
- confident defensive stance
- damage lands on armor while he remains annoyingly composed

### Patchadin the Overpowered

Visual identity:

- heavily armored holy-warrior parody
- polished plate
- absurdly excessive magical protection
- warm gold, ivory, royal blue and restrained radiant accents
- heroic pose pushed slightly past reasonable good taste
- visual joke comes from obvious over-buffing
