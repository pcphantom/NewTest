## Character Strategy
A disgustingly versatile holy warrior who embodies every ridiculous Paladin buff in MMORPG history. Patchadin can heal, tank, and DPS with equal proficiency because the developers clearly play this class. Through constant patches, hotfixes, and "emergency balance updates," this Paladin has achieved the ultimate status: being good at literally everything. Forum tears sustain them.

## Playstyle
- **Hybrid Supremacy**: Can adapt to any situation (damage, healing, defense)
- **Developer's Pet**: Survives when they shouldn't
- **Multi-Role Mastery**: Cards that do multiple things simultaneously
- **Patch-Powered**: Gets random buffs from constant updates
- **Unkillable Menace**: Defensive options that make opponents rage-quit

## Recommended Strategy

### Early Game (Turns 1-3)
1. **Establish Presence**: Crusader Strike for consistent damage
2. **Conserve Resources**: Hold Mighty Powers for later
3. **Assess Threats**: Use Can Do Everything to adapt
4. **Build Hand**: Patch Notes for advantage

### Mid Game (Turns 4-7)
1. **Apply Pressure**: Hammer of Wrath and Judgment
2. **Control Tempo**: Blessing of Kings for sustained power
3. **Stay Healthy**: Flash of Light as needed

### Late Game (Turns 8+)
1. **Power Plays**: Blatant Favoritism for efficiency
2. **All Paladin Raid**: Summon for sustained value
3. **Survive**: Bubble Hearth or Divine Intervention if threatened

## Design Philosophy

### Balance Considerations

**Strengths**: Highest versatility of any character, Can Do Everything is extremely powerful, strong in all game phases, Developer's Favorite gives an extra life, multiple win conditions

**Weaknesses**: No specialty (second-best at everything, best at nothing), medium damage ceiling, medium defense ceiling, requires skilled piloting, everyone wants to kill you because Paladins

**Power Level**: Tier 1 — top-tier competitive, versatility compensates for lack of specialization, high skill floor and very high skill ceiling

## Designer Commentary

**Purpose**: Parody MMORPG class balance issues, specifically notorious Paladin favoritism across multiple expansions.

**Mechanical Identity**: "Can Do Everything" literally lets you change card symbols, embodying the ridiculous versatility Paladins had at various points.

**Balance**: Despite being very strong, the deck lacks specialization. Good at everything, best at nothing specific. Requires skill to pilot optimally.

**Humor Target**: Anyone who's played an MMORPG and experienced constant class balance patches, flavor-of-the-month classes, developer favoritism, and forum complaints.

**Player Fantasy**: "What if I could play a character that's deliberately, hilariously overpowered while acknowledging it's ridiculous?"

**Recommended for players who enjoy**: MMORPG references, versatile adaptive gameplay, multiple win conditions, not being apologetic about being good, salt generation.

**Not recommended for players who**: Want specialized focused decks, prefer one strategy, take class balance very seriously.
