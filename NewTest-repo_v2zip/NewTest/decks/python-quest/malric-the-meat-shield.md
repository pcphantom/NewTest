# Malric the Meat Shield (Tank)
*Python's Quest for the Holy Kale Expansion*

**Status:** Version 1.0 — Ready for Playtesting
**Parody Target:** MMORPG tanking, specifically World of Warcraft

## Character Strategy
A professional damage absorber who studied at the prestigious School of Face-Tanking. Malric the Meat Shield specializes in forcing opponents to attack him while laughing off their damage with ridiculous amounts of armor, self-healing, and the sheer refusal to die. His mastery of MMORPG tanking mechanics — taunts, threat generation, and defensive cooldowns — makes him nearly unkillable when played correctly. Just don't ask him to do any actual damage.

## Playstyle
- **Threat Generation**: Force opponents to attack you
- **Damage Mitigation**: Stack shields and armor bonuses
- **Self-Sustaining**: Built-in healing from taking damage
- **Defensive Cooldowns**: "Oh crap" buttons for survival
- **Low Damage Output**: Can't kill quickly, but won't die either

## Special Mechanics

### Tank Theme
- **Taunt Mechanics**: Force opponents to target you
- **Threat Generation**: Passive effects that draw attacks
- **Damage Reduction**: Multiple layers of defense
- **Self-Healing**: Recover HP from tanking damage
- **Cooldown Management**: Powerful defensive abilities with limitations

### Sentinel Archetype
- **Protective Stance**: Benefits from being attacked
- **Aggro Management**: Control who attacks whom
- **Wall Strategy**: Become immovable object
- **Retaliation**: Counter-damage effects
- **Last Stand**: Most powerful when near death

## Tactical Notes

### Strengths
- **Incredible Durability**: Multiple shields and healing
- **Threat Control**: Forces opponents to attack suboptimally
- **Self-Sufficient**: Built-in healing from tanking
- **Shield Recursion**: Can replay shields via Cooldown Ready
- **Disruption Immunity**: Stand Your Ground prevents forced discards

### Weaknesses
- **Low Damage Output**: 7 attacks, lowest in game
- **Slow to Kill**: Takes forever to eliminate opponents
- **Vulnerable to Multi-Target**: Can't taunt area effects
- **Setup Dependent**: Needs shields in play to maximize value
- **Dead in Multiplayer**: Taunts don't work well in free-for-all

### Best Against
- **Single-Target Aggro**: Oriax, fast decks
- **Combo Decks**: Disrupts their setup time
- **Glass Cannons**: They can't break through shields
- **Direct Damage**: Absorbs it all day

### Vulnerable To
- **Area-of-Effect**: Can't taunt multi-target attacks
- **Defense Destruction**: Cards that destroy shields
- **Patient Control**: Decks that can wait you out
- **Poison/Burn**: Damage over time bypasses shields

## Recommended Strategy

### Early Game (Turns 1-3)
1. **Establish Defense**: Play Defensive Stance immediately
2. **Card Advantage**: Use Looking for Group or Tank Specs
3. **Threat Management**: Don't taunt yet, let them waste attacks elsewhere

### Mid Game (Turns 4-7)
1. **Taunt Priority Targets**: Use Aggressive Positioning on biggest threats
2. **Shield Stack**: Get multiple Defense cards in play
3. **Chip Damage**: Mocking Blow and Shield Bash for pressure
4. **Monitor HP**: Save Second Wind for critical moments

### Late Game (Turns 8+)
1. **Shield Wall**: When facing lethal threat
2. **Vengeance Combo**: Reflect damage while heavily defended
3. **Face Tank Loop**: Play Face Tank, let it break, heal, repeat
4. **Outlast**: You win by not dying, not by killing fast

## Advanced Combos

### The Immortal Combo
1. Play Shield Wall (6 shields, immune to ≤3 damage)
2. Play Vengeance (reflect damage when hit)
3. Taunt with Aggressive Positioning
4. Opponent attacks, takes reflected damage, barely hurts you
5. Next turn, play Cooldown Ready to return Shield Wall to hand

*Result: Near-invincibility with damage reflection*

### The Healing Tank Combo
1. Play Face Tank (4 shields with healing on break)
2. Play Block This! (2 shields with damage on break)
3. Opponent attacks, breaks both shields
4. You heal 3 HP and deal 2 damage

*Result: Gained health while dealing damage*

### The Card Advantage Engine
1. Play Looking for Group (draw 2)
2. Play Tank Specs (draw 2, buff shields)
3. Play Aggressive Positioning (taunt, will draw when hit)

*Result: +5 cards, +1 shield buff, forced opponent targeting*

### The Last Stand Combo
1. Get reduced to 4 HP or less
2. Second Wind triggers (heal 6 HP, draw 2)
3. Now at 10 HP with extra cards
4. Play Defensive Stance + Stand Your Ground

*Result: Dramatic comeback with protection*

### The Vengeance Loop
1. Stack multiple Defense cards (Defensive Stance x2, Stand Your Ground)
2. Play Vengeance (reflect damage)
3. Taunt opponent with Aggressive Positioning
4. They attack, barely damage you, take reflection
5. Repeat next turn

*Result: Slowly kill them with their own attacks*

## Design Philosophy

### Balance Considerations
**Strengths**: Extreme durability, self-sustaining through damage, forces suboptimal opponent plays, multiple defensive layers

**Weaknesses**: Very low damage output, slow to win, vulnerable to patience, taunts less useful in multiplayer

**Power Level**: Tier 1.5-2 — Strong in 1v1, weaker in free-for-all, skill-intensive timing, hard countered by specific strategies

### Tank Fantasy Checklist
- ✅ **High Survivability**: multiple shields, healing, damage reduction
- ✅ **Threat Management**: multiple taunt effects
- ⚠️ **Protecting Others**: N/A in free-for-all, but flavor text acknowledges this
- ✅ **Cooldown Management**: powerful defensive abilities with limitations
- ✅ **Self-Healing**: Face Tank, Second Wind, healing from tanking
- ✅ **Low Damage**: deliberately low offense
- ✅ **"Oh Crap" Buttons**: Shield Wall, Second Wind
- ✅ **Vengeance**: damage reflection ability

- **If too strong**: Reduce shield counts or healing amounts

## Designer Notes: Tank Archetype Design

### Challenges
- No allies to protect in free-for-all
- Low damage = very slow wins
- Defensive cards are inherently reactive
- Hard to make taunts relevant

### Solutions Used
- Taunts force suboptimal opponent plays
- Self-healing maintains pressure
- Counter-damage from shields
- Card advantage through survival
- Damage reflection mechanics

### Tournament Viability
- **1v1 Tournament**: Tier 1-1.5 (taunts work great)
- **Free-for-All**: Tier 2 (taunts less useful)
- **Team Format**: Tier 1 (actually has a team to protect)
- **Draft**: Tier 2 (needs specific cards)

**Skill Expression**: Low — just play shields (doesn't work). Medium — taunt timing, cooldown management. High — resource calculation, threat manipulation, knowing when to go offensive.
