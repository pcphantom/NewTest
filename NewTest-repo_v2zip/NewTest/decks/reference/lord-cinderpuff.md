# Lord Cinderpuff (Dragon)
*Monster Madness Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A defensive and wealthy dragon who builds up resources before unleashing fiery devastation. Lord Cinderpuff uses "Wall of Money" and other defensive cards to protect himself in the early game, then unleashes powerful attacks that can hit multiple targets. His corporate approach to destruction is both terrifying and oddly professional.

## Playstyle
- **Corporate Dragon**: Business-minded destruction
- **Defensive Buildup**: Turtle strategy with explosive payoff
- **Wealth Accumulation**: Resource-building focus
- **Multi-Target Attacks**: Area-of-effect devastation
- **Ancient Wisdom**: Strategic patience and planning

---

## Special Mechanics
### Corporate Theme
- **Business Terminology**: Hostile takeovers, liquidation, acquisitions
- **Wealth Defense**: Money-based protective abilities
- **Professional Approach**: Methodical, business-like combat
- **Legal Support**: Lawyer-based abilities

### Dragon Powers
- **Ancient Abilities**: Wisdom and experience-based effects
- **Fire Attacks**: Classic dragon breath and flame
- **Physical Dominance**: Tooth, claw, and wing attacks

## Tactical Notes
- **Strengths**: Strong defense buildup, powerful area attacks, resource accumulation
- **Weaknesses**: Slow early game, vulnerable during buildup phase
- **Best Against**: Aggressive rushdown strategies, single-target focused decks
- **Vulnerable To**: Sustained pressure, defense-bypassing abilities

## Recommended Strategy
1. **Early Defense**: Use Wall of Money to survive initial assault
2. **Resource Building**: Accumulate advantages through economic cards
3. **Patience Phase**: Wait for optimal moment to strike
4. **Explosive Endgame**: Unleash multiple area attacks
