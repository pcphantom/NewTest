# Hoots McGoots (Owlbear)
*Monster Madness Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A chaotic and entertaining performer whose deck is filled with cards that affect all players and often require physical actions, like dancing, from opponents in exchange for benefits. This makes Hoots McGoots a highly interactive and unpredictable character who brings party energy to every battle.

## Playstyle
- **Performance Art**: Theatrical combat approach
- **Interactive Chaos**: Cards requiring physical actions
- **All-Player Effects**: Cards that affect everyone
- **Entertainment Value**: Fun over pure efficiency
- **Unpredictable Actions**: Never know what's coming next

---

## Special Mechanics
### Performance Requirements
- **Physical Actions**: Some cards require opponents to dance, pose, etc.
- **Audience Participation**: Opponents can gain benefits by participating
- **Show Business**: Theatrical flair in combat
- **Interactive Elements**: Cards that engage all players

### Owlbear Nature
- **Wise as an Owl**: Intelligence-based abilities
- **Strong as a Bear**: Physical power abilities
- **Hybrid Creature**: Combination of traits

## Tactical Notes
- **Strengths**: Unpredictable effects, interactive fun, all-player benefits
- **Weaknesses**: Less focused strategy, opponent benefits from some cards
- **Best Against**: Serious, strategic players who get thrown off by chaos
- **Vulnerable To**: Players who ignore the theatrics and focus on damage

## Recommended Strategy
1. **Embrace Chaos**: Use unpredictability as an advantage
2. **Speed Setup**: Very Very Fast for positioning and tempo
3. **Interactive Benefits**: Use opponent participation to your advantage
4. **Grand Finale**: Save ultimate abilities for dramatic moments
