# Jaheira (Druid Shapeshifter)
*Battle for Baldur's Gate Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A complex and adaptable fighter who uses her unique Shapeshift ability to switch between offensive (Wolf Form) and defensive (Bear Form) stances, empowering her other cards with bonus effects. Her unique mechanic makes her challenging for beginners but highly rewarding for experienced players who master her form management.

## Playstyle
- **Shapeshift Mastery**: Transform between Bear and Wolf forms
- **Adaptive Strategy**: Forms enhance other cards with bonus effects
- **Nature Magic**: Druidic spells and nature-based attacks
- **Strategic Complexity**: Requires planning and form management
- **Versatile Combat**: Different approaches based on current form

---

## Special Mechanics
### Shapeshift Forms
- **Bear Form**: Defensive stance, enhances protective abilities
- **Wolf Form**: Offensive stance, enhances aggressive abilities
- **Form Management**: Only one form active at a time
- **Bonus Effects**: Other cards gain power when in matching form
- **Persistent State**: Forms remain until replaced by new form

## Form Strategy Guide

### Bear Form Strategy
- **Focus**: Defense and sustainability
- **Enhanced Cards**: Look for bear symbols on other cards
- **Best Time**: When under pressure or low on health
- **Synergies**: Defensive abilities gain bonus effects

### Wolf Form Strategy
- **Focus**: Aggressive offense and speed
- **Enhanced Cards**: Look for wolf symbols on other cards
- **Best Time**: When ready to press advantage
- **Synergies**: Attack cards gain bonus effects

## Tactical Notes
- **Strengths**: Incredible versatility, form-based bonuses, strategic depth
- **Weaknesses**: Complex to master, requires careful form timing
- **Best Against**: Players who can't adapt to changing strategies
- **Vulnerable To**: Simple, overwhelming aggression while learning

## Recommended Strategy
1. **Early Form**: Choose initial form based on opening hand
2. **Card Synergy**: Match forms to cards with bonus symbols
3. **Timing Switches**: Change forms when strategy needs to shift
4. **Bonus Maximization**: Plan turns around form-enhanced cards
