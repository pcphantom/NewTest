# Delilah Deathray (Beholder)
*Monster Madness Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A controlling and manipulative monster whose power comes from her deadly eye rays. Delilah's signature "Charm Ray" allows her to choose targets for all attacks in a round, turning opponents against each other. She can also unleash devastating board-wide attacks while maintaining her fabulous appearance.

## Playstyle
- **Eye Ray Mastery**: Multiple magical eye beam attacks
- **Mind Control**: Force opponents to attack each other
- **Board Control**: Area-of-effect devastation
- **Manipulative Tactics**: Turn enemies against themselves
- **Fashion Forward**: Style meets destruction

---

## Special Mechanics
### Charm Ray
- **Target Control**: Choose targets for all attacks in round
- **Friendly Fire**: Force opponents to attack each other
- **Strategic Manipulation**: Control battlefield flow
- **Duration**: Typically lasts until your next turn

### Eye Ray Varieties
- **Death Ray**: Ultimate destructive beam
- **Charm Ray**: Mind control beam
- **Multiple Rays**: Various magical effects
