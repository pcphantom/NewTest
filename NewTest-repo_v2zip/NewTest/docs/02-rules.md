# Concept Game Rules: Mini Multiplayer Offline RPG (MMORPG)
*A 2D Pixel-Art Card Battler for Dimensionally Challenged Heroes*

---

## Game Overview
**Welcome to the flattest RPG ever made!** This is a fast-paced, free-for-all card battler for 2-6 players where retro pixel-art heroes fight to be the last one standing. Drawing inspiration from classic TTRPG mechanics and vintage computer RPGs, players will use dice, strategic card play, Equipment upgrades, and wildly overpowered abilities to crush their opponents in gloriously two-dimensional combat.

## What Makes This Different?

### The 2D Revolution
- **Pixel Art Aesthetic**: Every character looks like they stepped out of a 1990s PC RPG
- **Self-Aware Cards**: Cards acknowledge their flat existence ("At least I'm not 1D!")
- **Retro Gaming Nostalgia**: Pool of Radiance meets modern card combat

### TTRPG Integration
- **Dice-Based Mechanics**: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 all have roles to play
- **Character Builds**: Tank, DPS, Support, Utility archetypes
- **Saving Throws**: Roll to resist effects or avoid damage
- **Critical Hits/Fails**: Natural 20s and natural 1s matter!
- **Equipment System**: Loot and upgrade your 2D hero!

### 4th Wall Breaking Humor
- Character names like "Malric the Meat Shield" or "Pixellia the Impossibly Flat"
- Cards with tongue-in-cheek flavor text about being 2D
- MMORPG terminology used ironically ("LFG for dungeon mayhem!")
- Equipment that references game mechanics directly

---

## Game Objective
**Last Pixel Standing**: Reduce all opponents to 0 HP through strategic card play, dice rolls, Equipment upgrades, and devastating specialty abilities. The final survivor claims victory in this Mini Multiplayer Offline RPG!

---

## Game Setup

### 1. Character Selection
Each player chooses a unique character deck (28 cards each). Characters represent different TTRPG/MMORPG archetypes:
- **Tanks** (High HP, defensive abilities, armor focus)
- **DPS** (Damage dealers with aggressive cards, weapon focus)
- **Support** (Healing, buffs, utility)
- **Controllers** (Disruption, manipulation)
- **Artificers** (Equipment specialists)
- **Hybrid Builds** (Mix of roles)

### 2. Player Components
Each player receives:
- Character Deck (28 cards, pre-constructed)
- HP Tracker (custom tracker with d12 slot)
- Starting HP Die (varies by character - most use d12, some may use d10 or d20)
- Character Reference Card
- Specialty Die (if character uses unique dice mechanics)
- Equipment Zone (space for persistent Equipment cards)

### 3. Set Starting Health
- **Standard**: Roll your character's HP die (typically d12) - that's your starting HP
- **Alternative**: All players start at maximum (12 HP for d12 characters, etc.)
- **Hardcore Mode**: Roll d12 regardless of character, start with that HP

### 4. Prepare Deck
- Shuffle your 28-card character deck
- Place face-down as your Draw Pile
- Keep Discard Pile space nearby

### 5. Draw Opening Hand
Draw 3 cards from your Draw Pile

### 6. Determine First Player
- **Classic**: Youngest player goes first
- **TTRPG Style**: Everyone rolls d20, highest goes first (reroll ties)
- **Speed Run**: Fastest to shuffle goes first
- Play proceeds clockwise

## Turn Structure

### Phase 1: Draw Phase
Draw **one card** from your Draw Pile
- Equipment may modify this (e.g., "Rendering Engine +1" draws 2 instead)

### Phase 2: Play Phase
- **Must** play at least one card from your hand
- May play additional cards if granted by "Play Again" (⚡) effects
- **Actions are mandatory** - you cannot skip playing if you have actions available
- Some cards may require dice rolls to resolve effects
- Equipment effects apply automatically when relevant cards are played

### Phase 3: Discard Phase
- All played cards go to your Discard Pile
- **Exception**: Defense cards, Equipment, and persistent effects remain in play

### Phase 4: End Phase
- Resolve any "end of turn" effects
- Equipment bonuses continue into next turn
- Turn passes clockwise to next player

## Core Mechanics: The Five Pillars

### ⚔️ Attack (Sword Symbol)
- Each sword symbol = 1 point of damage
- **Equipment bonus**: Add Weapon bonuses to total damage
- Choose a single opponent as target (unless card specifies otherwise)
- Damage hits Defense cards first, then HP
- Excess damage "spills over" to remaining Defense cards or HP

**Dice-Based Attacks**:
- Some attacks deal variable damage (e.g., "Deal 1d4 damage")
- Roll the specified die when playing the card
- **Equipment applies after roll**: "1d4+2" means roll d4, then add your Weapon bonus
- Critical hits (max roll) may have bonus effects if specified on card

### 🛡️ Defense (Shield Symbol)
- Remains in play when played (doesn't discard immediately)
- Each shield symbol = 1 point of damage absorption
- **Equipment bonus**: Add Armor bonuses to shield count
- Place damage tokens on shield symbols when hit
- Destroyed when all symbols are covered
- **You cannot lose HP while you have active Defense cards with undamaged shields**

**Special Defense Mechanics**:
- Some shields require minimum damage to break (e.g., "Laminated Armor: Immune to 1-damage attacks")
- Some shields trigger effects when broken
- Shields can have durations ("Until your next turn")

### ❤️ Healing (Heart Symbol)
- Each heart symbol = 1 Hit Point restored
- **Cannot exceed your maximum HP** (determined by your HP die)
- Some healing may be dice-based (e.g., "Heal 1d6 HP")
- Equipment may enhance healing effects

### 🃏 Draw (Card with Plus Symbol)
- Draw one card from your Draw Pile per symbol
- Equipment may grant passive card draw
- Some effects let you draw from specific piles or opponents' decks

### ⚡ Play Again (Lightning Bolt Symbol)
- Grants one additional action per symbol
- **Actions are mandatory** - you must play another card if able
- Can chain multiple Play Again effects for devastating combos
- Some Equipment modifies Play Again effects

## NEW MECHANIC: Dice Integration

### Standard Dice Usage
- **d4**: Minor variable effects (1-4 damage/healing)
- **d6**: Moderate variable effects (1-6 damage/healing)
- **d8**: Significant variable effects
- **d10**: High-power variable effects
- **d12**: Maximum HP for most characters
- **d20**: Saving throws, critical mechanics

### Saving Throws
Some cards force opponents to make saving throws:
- Target rolls d20
- Card specifies DC (Difficulty Class) to beat
- **Success**: Reduced or negated effect
- **Failure**: Full effect applies
- **Natural 20**: Always succeeds, may have bonus benefit
- **Natural 1**: Always fails, may have penalty

**Example**: "Vengeful Spirit - Target player makes DC 12 saving throw or takes 3 damage"

### Critical Mechanics
- **Natural 20**: Maximum effect + bonus if card specifies
- **Natural 1**: Minimum effect, may backfire if card specifies
- **Equipment may modify crit range**: Some items let you crit on 19-20

## NEW MECHANIC: Equipment & Items

### How Equipment Works

#### Playing Equipment
- Equipment cards are played like any other card (cost 1 action)
- Once played, they remain in play in your Equipment Zone
- Effects are **passive** and apply automatically to relevant cards
- Equipment bonuses stack unless card says otherwise

#### Equipment Limits
- **Standard**: No limit on total Equipment
- **Slot System** (Optional): 1 Weapon, 1 Armor, 2 Accessories max
- **Overloaded** (Variant): More than 3 Equipment = take 1 damage per turn (too much stuff!)

### Equipment Types

#### Weapons (Damage Modifiers)
These add damage to your attack cards:

**2D-Themed Examples**:
- **"Paper Cutter +1" (Scissor Sword)**: All attacks deal +1 damage. *"Cuts through the competition... literally."*
- **"Pixel Blade +2"**: All attacks deal +2 damage. *"Sharpened to exactly 1 pixel width."*
- **"Cardboard Tube of Destiny"**: Roll d4 when attacking, add result to damage. *"Surprisingly effective."*
- **"Flatland Falchion +1"**: All attacks deal +1 damage, +2 if target has Defense cards. *"Specially designed for thin combat."*
- **"2D6 of Doom"**: Once per turn, roll 2d6 instead of normal attack damage. *"Why 2d6? Because we're 2D, obviously."*
- **"Rusty Spoon +1"**: All attacks deal +1 damage, but you must narrate your attack dramatically. *"It's the rules."*
- **"Render Distance Bow +2"**: All attacks deal +2 damage. Can't attack adjacent players. *"I can only see far away."*

#### Armor (Defense Modifiers)
These enhance your defensive capabilities:

**2D-Themed Examples**:
- **"0.3mm Shield +1"**: All Defense cards have +1 shield symbol. *"Technically the thickest shield in the 2D realm."*
- **"Laminated Armor"**: Defense cards can't be destroyed by attacks dealing 1 damage. *"Now with protective coating!"*
- **"Portrait Frame"**: First Defense card each turn has +2 shields. *"You're a work of art, literally."*
- **"Anti-Folding Vest"**: When you play a Defense card, draw a card. *"Stay rigid, stay alive."*
- **"Stack Overflow Shield"**: When Defense card is destroyed, place 1 shield on another Defense card. *"Redirect the damage buffer!"*
- **"Cardstock Chestplate +1"**: All Defense cards have +1 shield. Take 1 damage when you draw a card. *"Heavy armor is heavy."*
- **"Transparency Cloak"**: Opponents must roll d20 (DC 10) to target you with attacks. *"I'm see-through! Wait, that's bad for hiding..."*

#### Accessories (Special Abilities)
These grant unique passive effects:

**2D-Themed Examples**:
- **"Rendering Engine +1"**: Draw an extra card at start of your turn. *"Faster FPS = faster gameplay."*
- **"Save State Gem"**: Once per game, when reduced to 0 HP, return to 5 HP instead. *"Quicksave before boss battle? Obviously."*
- **"Low-Res Goggles"**: Opponents must roll d20 to successfully target you (DC 12). *"I can't see them, they can't see me."*
- **"Compression Amulet"**: Your hand size limit is 8 instead of unlimited. Wait, there's no hand limit. *"But it looks cool!"*
- **"Refresh Rate Ring"**: ⚡ Play Again effects let you draw 1 card before playing your next card. *"Smooth 60 FPS gameplay."*
- **"4th Wall Breaker"**: Once per turn, you may ignore a Mighty Power targeting you. *"I can see the card text!"*
- **"Loading Bar Hourglass"**: First card you play each turn has ⚡ Play Again. Others take twice as long. *"Buffering..."*
- **"Screenshot Trophy"**: Whenever you eliminate a player, gain +1 to all Equipment bonuses. *"Achievement Unlocked!"*

#### Consumables (One-Time Power)
These are powerful but disappear after use:

**2D-Themed Examples**:
- **"Healing Potion (Flat)"**: Heal 1d6+2 HP immediately, then discard. *"It's all surface area anyway."*
- **"Polygon Remover"**: Destroy target Equipment card. *"Simplifying the geometry."*
- **"Resolution Increase"**: This turn, all your attacks deal double damage. *"Going from 8-bit to 16-bit!"*
- **"Debug Mode Scroll"**: Look at target opponent's hand, steal one card. *"Console commands enabled."*
- **"Sprite Swap Token"**: Exchange HP totals with target opponent. *"Character swap glitch!"*
- **"Respec Potion"**: Return all your Equipment to hand, draw 3 cards. *"Time to try a new build."*
- **"Lag Bomb"**: Target opponent skips their next turn. *"Connection timeout."*
- **"Clip Through Walls"**: Play this when targeted by an attack; negate all damage. *"Out of bounds exploit!"*

### Equipment Destruction

#### How Equipment Gets Destroyed
- **Direct Destruction**: Some cards specifically destroy Equipment
- **Sacrifice**: Player may discard Equipment for powerful effect
- **Overload**: Optional rule where too much Equipment causes problems
- **Curse/Debuff**: Some debuffs destroy or disable Equipment

**Equipment Destruction Cards Examples**:
- **"System Crash"**: Destroy all Equipment cards in play. *"Have you tried turning it off and on again?"*
- **"Geometry Reducer"**: Destroy target Equipment, draw 2 cards. *"Simplifying your existence."*
- **"Rust Spray"** (Weapon): Deal 2 damage, destroy target Weapon. *"Even pixels corrode."*
- **"Format C:"**: Destroy all your Equipment, deal 3 damage per Equipment destroyed. *"Scorched earth tactics."*

### Equipment Strategy

#### Equipment-Focused Builds
Some characters are designed around Equipment synergy:

**"Pixelsmith" (Artificer)**:
- Starts with 2 Equipment in opening hand
- Can play 2 Equipment cards per turn
- Signature ability: "Forge" - Discard 2 cards, create Equipment token with +1 of your choice

**"The Hoarder" (Packrat Rogue)**:
- No Equipment limit (or very high limit)
- Gains benefits for each Equipment (draw cards, deal damage)
- Signature ability: "Everything But The Kitchen Sink" - Deal 1 damage per Equipment you control

**"Minimalist Monk"**:
- Cannot play Equipment cards
- Gains massive bonuses for having NO Equipment
- Signature ability: "Unencumbered" - +2 damage and +2 shields for each Equipment slot you DON'T use (up to 4 slots = +8!)

**"Gearhead Gadgeteer"**:
- All Equipment has double bonuses
- Can only have 2 Equipment total
- Signature ability: "Overclock" - Double one Equipment's bonus until your next turn, then destroy it

### Equipment Combos

#### Synergy Examples
Equipment can create powerful combinations:

**Offensive Combo**:
- Paper Cutter +1 (weapon)
- Resolution Increase (consumable)
- Refresh Rate Ring (accessory)
= Attack for (base damage +1) × 2, then draw before next play

**Defensive Combo**:
- 0.3mm Shield +1 (armor)
- Portrait Frame (armor)
- Stack Overflow Shield (armor)
= First Defense each turn has +3 shields total, redistributes damage when broken

**Degenerate Combo** (intentionally powerful):
- Rendering Engine +1 (draw extra)
- 4th Wall Breaker (ignore Mighty Power)
- Save State Gem (cheat death)
= Nearly unkillable card advantage engine

**Glass Cannon Combo**:
- Pixel Blade +2 (weapon)
- Render Distance Bow +2 (weapon)
- Cardboard Tube of Destiny (weapon)
= Potentially +4 to +6 damage per attack, but no defense!

### Equipment Humor & Flavor

#### 2D-Specific Equipment Jokes
- **"Third Dimension (Contraband)"**: Illegal in 2D space. If caught, discard and take 5 damage. *"Reported to authorities."*
- **"Depth Perception Training"**: Does literally nothing because you're 2D. *"You tried."*
- **"The Actual Third Dimension"**: This card is slightly embossed. That's it. +0 to everything. *"But it feels special!"*
- **"Fold Line"**: Can be bent to create 3D effect. Counts as 2 shields while folded, but you must hold the card at 90° angle. *"Origami tactics."*
- **"Z-Axis Modifier"**: Adds depth to your character. Cannot be played. *"Incompatible with 2D systems."*

#### Meta-Humor Equipment
- **"Nerf Bat"**: Reduce target's Weapon bonus by 2. *"Patch notes: This card was too strong, so we made it weaker."*
- **"Buff Banner"**: Increase your Armor bonus by 1. *"Patch notes: This card was too weak, so we made it stronger."*
- **"Power Creep"**: Each turn this is in play, it gains +1 bonus to everything. *"By turn 5, this will be banned."*
- **"Premium Currency"**: Pay $5 real money to draw 5 cards. *"Just kidding, we're not THAT kind of game... unless?"*
- **"Season Pass"**: Costs 3 actions to play. Does nothing. *"But you get a sense of pride and accomplishment!"*
- **"Loot Box"**: Roll d20. 1-10: Nothing. 11-19: Draw 2 cards. 20: Draw 5 cards and heal 5 HP. *"It's not gambling if it's pixels!"*

### Equipment Design Philosophy

#### Balance Considerations
- Equipment should feel **impactful** but not **game-breaking**
- +1 bonuses are common, +2 are rare, +3 are legendary/unique
- Consumables should be stronger than permanent Equipment
- Some Equipment has drawbacks to balance power
- Destruction effects keep Equipment from dominating
- Characters with fewer Equipment slots get stronger individual items

#### Thematic Integration
- Every Equipment name should reference 2D nature, retro gaming, or MMORPG culture
- Flavor text should break 4th wall or make meta-jokes
- Visual design: Equipment cards have different border (gear/cog pattern?)
- Equipment should feel like RPG loot drops
- Rarity tiers: Common (grey), Uncommon (green), Rare (blue), Epic (purple), Legendary (orange)

## Specialty Card Types

### Standard Types (from original)
- **Attack Cards**: Deal damage
- **Defense Cards**: Prevent damage
- **Healing Cards**: Restore HP
- **Draw Cards**: Card advantage
- **Utility Cards**: Various effects
- **Mighty Powers**: Unique, powerful abilities

### NEW: Advanced Card Types

#### Equipment Cards
- Remain in play providing persistent bonuses
- Can be destroyed by specific effects
- Stack for cumulative benefits
- Thematically embrace 2D/retro/meta humor

#### Summon Cards
- Remain in play as separate entities
- Have their own HP and actions
- Act on your turn or independently
- Can be targeted and destroyed
- Examples: Imp (1 HP, 1 damage/turn), Skeleton (2 HP, defend you), Familiar (0 HP, draw cards)

#### Form/Stance Cards
- Persistent state changes
- Modify how other cards work
- Only one active at a time
- New form replaces old form
- Examples: Bear Form (defense boost), Wolf Form (offense boost), Shadow Form (evasion)

#### Reaction Cards
- Can be played on opponent's turn
- Trigger in response to specific events
- Limited number per character
- Examples: Counterspell (negate Mighty Power), Dodge Roll (avoid attack), Interrupt (stop Play Again chain)

#### Curse/Debuff Cards
- Attach to opponent
- Create ongoing negative effects
- May require saving throws to remove
- Examples: Poison (1 damage per turn), Slow (draw 1 less card), Hex (Equipment doesn't work)

## Hit Points & Elimination

### HP Tracking
- Track HP on your HP Tracker using your character's die
- **Most characters**: d12 (12 max HP)
- **Tanks/Beefy builds**: d20 (20 max HP)
- **Glass cannons**: d10 (10 max HP)
- **Weird builds**: d8 (8 max HP, but powerful abilities)

### Elimination
- Reduced to 0 HP = immediately eliminated from the game
- Remove all your cards from play
- **Equipment drops**: Optional rule where eliminated players' Equipment scatters (opponents roll to claim)
- **Optional**: Become Vengeful Ghost (see Expansion Mechanics)

### Victory
- **Last player standing wins**
- **Simultaneous elimination**: Tied players each roll their HP die - highest wins the tie
- **No survivors**: The 2D realm collapses. Start new game.

## Contingency Rules: The "Mayhem" Factor

### Empty Hand Rule
If forced to play but hand is empty:
1. Draw 2 cards immediately
2. Must play one of them

### Empty Deck Rule
If you need to draw but Draw Pile is empty:
1. Shuffle your Discard Pile
2. Place face-down as new Draw Pile
3. **No penalty** for running out

### Impossible Action Rule
If card effect cannot be legally resolved:
- Card is still played and discarded
- Do as much of the effect as possible
- Continue turn normally
- Example: "Draw 3 cards" but deck only has 1 card - draw the 1 card

### Equipment Overload Rule (Optional)
If you have more than 3 Equipment:
- Take 1 damage at end of your turn per excess Equipment
- Example: 5 Equipment = take 2 damage per turn
- Prevents infinite Equipment stacking

## Advanced Mechanics

### Persistent Effects
Some cards remain in play beyond a single turn:
- **Defense cards**: Until destroyed by damage
- **Equipment**: Until destroyed or discarded
- **Summons/Minions**: Until destroyed or dismissed
- **Form cards**: Until replaced by another form
- **Buffs**: May specify duration ("Until your next turn")

### Forced Actions
- All actions granted by ⚡ Play Again are mandatory
- You must play a card if you have one and an action
- If effect forces opponent to do something, they must comply if able

### Targeting Priority
- **Standard attacks**: Attacker chooses target
- **Area effects**: Hit all legal targets simultaneously
- **Forced targeting**: Card effect overrides normal choice
- **Zone of Influence** (5-6 players): Can only target adjacent opponents unless card says otherwise

### Card Text Hierarchy
**Golden Rule**: Specific card text always overrides general rules
- If card contradicts rulebook, card wins
- If two cards conflict, active player's card takes precedence
- Equipment modifiers apply after base card effects
- **"Cannot" effects override "can" effects**

## Multiplayer Expansion Mechanics

### Zone of Influence (5-6 Players)
- Can only target opponents **directly adjacent** (left/right) with standard attacks
- **Does not restrict**: Area effects, Mighty Powers, or cards specifying "any opponent"
- Prevents early-game focus fire
- **Ranged Equipment**: Some Equipment ignores adjacency requirement

### Vengeful Ghosts (Optional Rule)
When eliminated, players may become Vengeful Ghosts:
- **On your turn**: Deal 1 unavoidable damage to any active player (roll d20 vs DC 10 to resist)
- **Cannot eliminate**: Can reduce to 1 HP, not to 0
- **Duration**: Until one player remains, then you're just dead
- **Ghost Equipment**: Cannot use Equipment while dead (obviously)

### Hardcore Mode
- All players start with rolled HP (no maximum start)
- All variable damage rolls twice, take higher result
- Saving throw failures deal additional 1 damage
- First blood: Player who deals first damage draws 2 cards
- **Permadeath Equipment**: When you die, tear up your Equipment cards. Permanently.

### Team Deathmatch (2v2 or 3v3)
- Teammates sit across from each other
- Cannot directly attack teammates
- Can play beneficial cards on teammates
- Team shares victory condition
- **Shared Equipment**: Optional rule where teammates can "borrow" each other's Equipment

## Dice Rolling Guidelines

### When to Roll
- **Variable Damage/Healing**: Roll when card is played
- **Saving Throws**: Roll when targeted by effect
- **On-Hit Effects**: Roll after damage is confirmed to hit
- **Equipment Procs**: Some Equipment requires rolls

### Roll Modifiers
Some cards grant advantage/disadvantage (TTRPG style):
- **Advantage**: Roll twice, take higher result
- **Disadvantage**: Roll twice, take lower result
- Some Equipment grants advantage on specific rolls

### Critical Success/Failure
- **Natural 20** (on d20): Automatic success, double variable effects
- **Natural 1** (on d20): Automatic failure, may cause backfire
- **Equipment may modify**: Some items let you crit on 19-20 or avoid fails on 2

### Dice Stacking (Optional Rule)
- When you roll the same number on multiple dice, effects stack
- Example: Roll 2d6, both show "4" - effect triggers twice
- Mostly for flavor and excitement

## Designer Notes

### Balance Philosophy
- **Aggressive > Defensive**: Games should be fast and furious (15-25 minutes)
- **High Variance Acceptable**: Dice adds chaos, embrace it
- **Unique > Balanced**: Characters should feel wildly different
- **Combo Potential**: Reward clever card combinations
- **Mandatory Actions**: Prevent turtling and stalling
- **Equipment Matters**: Items should significantly impact strategy
- **Destruction Exists**: Equipment can be destroyed to prevent runaway leads

### Equipment Balance
- Basic Equipment (+1) should be common in most decks
- Strong Equipment (+2/+3) should be rare and contestable
- Consumables should be powerful but one-time
- Every Equipment-heavy character should have weaknesses
- Destruction effects prevent Equipment dominance
- Glass cannon builds (all weapon, no armor) should be viable but risky

### Dice Philosophy
- Dice add excitement and variance
- Failed rolls shouldn't feel bad (consolation prizes on cards)
- Critical successes should feel AMAZING
- Natural 1s should be funny, not frustrating
- Average rolls should still accomplish something

### Humor Philosophy
- Every card should have personality
- Flavor text > mechanical clarity (within reason)
- 4th wall breaks are encouraged
- Self-deprecating humor about being 2D
- Gentle mockery of MMORPG/TTRPG tropes
- Never mean-spirited, always fun

### Future Expansion Ideas
- **Multiclassing**: Combine two character decks (56 cards, choose 28)
- **Dungeon Tiles**: Environmental effects that change gameplay
- **PvE Boss Decks**: Cooperative mode against overpowered AI
- **Team-based Gameplay**: Dedicated 2v2 characters
- **Tournament Rules**: Banlist, draft format
- **Legacy/Campaign**: Characters level up between games, unlock new Equipment
- **Equipment Crafting**: Combine basic Equipment to create powerful combos
- **Randomizer Mode**: Each player gets random Equipment at start
- **Raid Boss Mode**: 4 players vs 1 mega-buffed boss with 50+ HP

- **Accessory Slot 2**: Special abilities

### Common Equipment Bonuses
- **+1**: Common, most characters have access
- **+2**: Rare, powerful but balanced
- **+3**: Legendary, one per deck maximum
- **+1d4**: Variable, exciting rolls
- **Special Effects**: Unique abilities that don't use +numbers

### Equipment Destruction Methods
- Direct destroy cards: "Destroy target Equipment"
- Area destroy: "Destroy all Equipment"
- Sacrifice: Voluntary discard for benefit
- Conditional: "When you take 5 damage, destroy one Equipment"
- Overload: Automatic discard if too many

### Equipment Strategy Tips
1. **Don't over-commit**: Diversify between Weapons/Armor/Accessories
2. **Protect key Equipment**: Some decks counter Equipment-heavy strategies
3. **Timing matters**: When to play Equipment vs when to hold it
4. **Consumables for clutch moments**: Save them for game-winning plays
5. **Destruction is real**: Don't assume Equipment stays forever
