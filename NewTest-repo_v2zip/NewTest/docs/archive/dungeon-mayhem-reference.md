# Dungeon Mayhem: Rules & Gameplay Mechanics

## Game Objective
**The Last Adventurer Standing**: A free-for-all battle for 2-6 players where the goal is to be the last player remaining by reducing all other players' Hit Points to zero.

## Game Setup
1. **Character Selection**: Each player chooses a unique character deck
2. **Player Components**: Take Hit Point Tracker, Hit Point Token, and Reference Card
3. **Set Starting Health**: Place Hit Point Token on 10 (all players start with 10 HP)
4. **Prepare Deck**: Shuffle your 28-card character deck, place face down as Draw Pile
5. **Draw Opening Hand**: Draw 3 cards from your Draw Pile
6. **Determine First Player**: Youngest player goes first, then clockwise

## Turn Structure
### 1. Draw Phase
Draw one card from your Draw Pile

### 2. Play Phase
- **Must** play at least one card from your hand
- May play additional cards if granted extra actions by "Play Again" effects
- Actions are **mandatory** - you cannot choose to skip playing if you have actions

### 3. Discard Phase
- All played cards go to your Discard Pile
- **Exception**: Defense cards remain in play

### 4. End Phase
Turn passes to the next player (clockwise)

## Core Mechanics: The Five Pillars

### ⚔️ Attack (Sword Symbol)
- Each sword = 1 point of damage
- Choose a single opponent as target
- Damage hits Defense cards first, then Hit Points
- Excess damage "spills over" to other Defense cards

### 🛡️ Defense (Shield Symbol)
- Remains in play when played (doesn't go to discard)
- Each shield symbol = 1 point of damage absorption
- Place damage tokens on shield symbols when damaged
- Destroyed when all symbols are covered
- **Cannot lose HP while you have active Defense cards**

### ❤️ Healing (Heart Symbol)
- Each heart = 1 Hit Point restored
- Maximum HP is 10 (excess healing is lost)

### 🃏 Draw (Card with Plus Symbol)
- Draw one card from your Draw Pile per symbol

### ⚡ Play Again (Lightning Bolt Symbol)
- Grants one additional action per symbol
- **Actions are mandatory** - you must play another card
- Can force suboptimal plays if hand management is poor

## Hit Points & Elimination
- Start with 10 HP, tracked on Hit Point Tracker
- Reduced to 0 HP = immediately eliminated
- Healing cannot exceed 10 HP
- Last player standing wins

## Contingency Rules: The "Mayhem" Factor

### Empty Hand Rule
If forced to play a card but hand is empty:
1. Immediately draw 2 cards from Draw Pile
2. Continue turn by playing one of them

### Empty Deck Rule
If need to draw but Draw Pile is empty:
1. Take Discard Pile and shuffle it
2. Place face down as new Draw Pile
3. **No penalty** for running out of cards

## Mighty Powers
- Unique text-based abilities specific to each character
- **Card text overrides standard rules**
- Create dramatic, game-altering moments
- Define each character's unique playstyle

## Expansion Mechanics

### Battle for Baldur's Gate

#### Jaheira's Shapeshift
- **Form Cards**: "Shapeshift: Bear Form" and "Shapeshift: Wolf Form"
- Forms remain in play (persistent state)
- Other cards gain bonus effects while in specific Forms
- **Only one Form active at a time** - new Form replaces old one

#### Minsc & Boo's Stolen Cards
- Cards taken from opponents are "stolen"
- After playing stolen card, return to **original owner's** Discard Pile
- If Minsc & Boo eliminated, return all stolen cards to owners

### Monster Madness Multiplayer Rules

#### Zone of Influence (5-6 Players)
- Can only target opponents **directly to your left and right** with standard Attacks
- **Does not apply** to Mighty Powers or effects targeting "each opponent"
- Prevents unfair focus fire and early elimination

#### Vengeful Ghosts (Optional Rule)
- Eliminated players can become Vengeful Ghosts
- On their turn: Deal 1 unavoidable damage to any active player
- **Cannot deal final damage** - can reduce to 1 HP but not eliminate

## Advanced Rulings

### Forced Actions
- If a card effect cannot be resolved, still must play the card
- Effect fails, card is discarded normally
- All actions are mandatory unless card says "you may"

### Targeting
- Unless restricted, player performing action chooses target
- Includes actions forced by opponent's cards

### Timing
- "Until your next turn" effects expire at start of next turn (before Draw Phase)
- Card text is literal - interpret exactly as written

## Win Conditions
- **Victory**: Last player standing
- **Tie**: All remaining players eliminated simultaneously
- **Tie Resolution**: Play another game!

---

*The game emphasizes fast-paced, direct conflict with spectacular moments over resource management. The "mayhem" comes from powerful character abilities and the mandatory nature of actions creating unpredictable chain reactions.*
