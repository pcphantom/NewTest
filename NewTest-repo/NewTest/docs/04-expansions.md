# Expansions

Each expansion is its own parody universe, built on identical mechanics. That's the engine of the whole project: the rules never change, so any deck can play against any other deck, while the humor can go anywhere.

---

## The structure

- **4-6 character decks** per expansion
- Each deck is 28 cards, 12 HP, standard symbols
- Shared theme cards appear across multiple decks within an expansion
- Every card carries four layers at once:
  1. **Primary effect** — the mechanics
  2. **Genre reference** — the parody target
  3. **Modern twist** — tech/internet culture angle
  4. **4th-wall break** — acknowledging it's a card

Worked example, "404 Error — Card Not Found":
- *Primary:* remove a card from play
- *Genre:* absurdist bureaucracy, things inexplicably not working
- *Modern:* the HTTP error everyone recognizes
- *4th wall:* the card says it can't be found, while being a card

## Expansion 1 — Python's Quest for the Holy Kale

**Status:** in development. Three decks complete.

**Premise:** computer programs gained sentience and discovered the source of all Karen power — the mystical Holy Kale. Now they quest to control it, debug reality, and finally understand why humans keep turning them off and on again.

**Tone:** absurdist comedy meets modern tech culture. Bureaucratic nonsense, wellness-industry satire, error messages as weapons.

### Drafted signature cards

**Moot Groundsplatter** *("I got better... repeatedly.")*
- **Not Quite Dead** ×3 — when reduced to 0 HP, return to 5 HP instead
- **Flesh Wound** ×2 — when you take 5+ damage, reduce it to 1
- **Limbless Wonder** ×2 — 🛡️🛡️🛡️; when broken, deal 2 damage to the attacker
- **Invincible? Probably.** — heal 1d4 whenever you survive lethal damage

**Unladen Swallow** *("African or European? THE ANSWER MATTERS.")*
- **Airspeed Velocity** ×3 — ⚡⚡
- **The Favorite Colour Question** ×2 — ask an opponent their favorite color. Whatever they say is wrong. They fall off a hill and take 3 damage.
- **Coconut Debate** ×2 — target skips their next action while arguing about migratory patterns
- **Carried by Whom?** — steal target's Equipment

**Sir Syntax Error** *("Expected semicolon, found chaos.")*
- **Syntax Error** ×3 — this card does nothing. (However, you may hurl it at a small furry animal for 4 damage.)
- **Null Pointer Exception** ×2 — remove target from the game; they return at end of round
- **Segmentation Fault** ×2 — deal 3 damage. Roll d6: on a 1, deal it to yourself instead.
- **Core Dumped** — deal all your remaining HP as damage, then heal to full
- **Stack Overflow** — copy the last card played by any player

**404 — Karen Not Found** *("I demand to speak to whoever programmed this reality.")*
- **404 Error — Card Not Found** ×2 — choose one: remove target card from play, OR force target to shuffle hand into deck and redraw the same number, OR target skips their turn
- **Speak to Your Manager** ×3 — summon a Manager (2 HP, 1 damage/turn); opponents must attack it first
- **Holy Kale Smoothie** ×2 — heal 1d6; opponents make a DC 12 save or skip their turn raving about wellness culture
- **Essential Oils Attack** — 1 damage to all opponents; they can't heal next turn
- **Call Corporate** — destroy all Equipment in play

**Tim the Enchanter** *("There are some who call me... overpowered.")*
- **Fireballs! Fireballs!** ×3 — deal 5 damage, take 2 from excitement
- **Anticlimactic Introduction** ×2 — build up a dramatic entrance, deal 1 damage
- **That Rabbit Has a Mean Streak** ×2 — target "harmless" player reveals they deal 6 damage
- **The Counting Grenade** ×1 — count to 3 (not 2, not 4, 3). Deal 8 damage.

**README.txt** *("Nobody ever reads me, but I have all the answers.")*
- **RTFM** ×3 — 🃏🃏
- **Ignored Warnings** ×2 — when an opponent plays an Attack, reveal this: they take their own damage instead
- **Deprecated Function** ×2 — cancel target ability
- **Patch Notes** — heal all players 2 HP, then deal 4 damage to one
- **Known Issues** — target takes 1 damage per Equipment they have

### Shared theme cards

Available across multiple decks in this expansion:

- **Run Away!** — return to starting HP but discard your hand
- **'Tis But a Scratch** — 🛡️🛡️; when broken, deal 1 damage back
- **Shrubbery Required** — if you have 3 Equipment, draw 3 cards
- **The Taunting Frenchman** — deal 1 damage; opponent takes 1 additional damage from sheer frustration
- **Constitutional Peasant** — reaction: when targeted, all players draw 1 card
- **Knights Who Say a Single Syllable** — summon a 3 HP creature that demands Equipment; opponent sacrifices Equipment or takes 2 damage/turn
- **It's Only a Model** — 🛡️🛡️🛡️🛡️ but occupies 2 Equipment slots

### Modern tech cards

- **Turn It Off and On Again** — destroy all persistent effects; everyone draws 2
- **Password Reset** — target forgets their abilities for 1 turn
- **Captcha Failed** — opponent must identify traffic lights in abstract pixel art. They always fail. Skip turn.
- **Terms and Conditions** — deal 10 damage. Takes five minutes to read. Nobody does.
- **Software Update** — heal 3 HP but skip your next turn. *"Installing... 47 hours remaining."*
- **Free Trial Expired** — destroy target Equipment

### Equipment

- **Coconut Halves** (+0) — make horse sounds. Grants ⚡.
- **The Counting Grenade** (+3, consumable) — one use, 8 damage after counting to 3
- **Vorpal Bunny** (+2) — looks harmless, double damage on first attack
- **Shrubbery (A Nice One)** (+1) — +1 to everything
- **Tinned Mystery Meat** — heal 1 HP per turn. Everyone groans.
- **The Bridge of Questions** — opponents answer questions or take 3 damage

### Boss deck (PvE mode)

**The Holy Kale Itself** — 50 HP, radiates Karen energy, demands to speak to players' managers, gluten-free damage type, farm-to-table attack pattern.
