# Oriax the Clever (Rogue)
*Base Game Character*

## Character Strategy
An agile and relentless attacker whose deck is packed with a high volume of attack cards. Oriax overwhelms opponents with a flurry of thrown daggers and possesses thieving abilities to steal potions (heal) and pick pockets (draw cards) from his foes, representing the classic rogue archetype.

## Playstyle
- **Dagger Mastery**: High volume of throwing weapons
- **Relentless Assault**: Overwhelming attack frequency
- **Thievery Skills**: Steal resources from opponents
- **Agile Combat**: Quick, precise strikes
- **Resource Denial**: Disrupt opponent strategies through theft

## Tactical Notes
- **Strengths**: Consistent damage output, resource disruption, reliable attacks
- **Weaknesses**: Limited defense options, relies on sustained assault
- **Best Against**: Defensive strategies, resource-dependent characters

## Recommended Strategy
1. **Sustained Pressure**: Maintain constant dagger attacks
2. **Resource Theft**: Use Pick Pocket to disrupt opponents
3. **Timing Strikes**: Save Sneak Attack for crucial moments
4. **Overwhelm Defense**: Use volume of attacks to break through
