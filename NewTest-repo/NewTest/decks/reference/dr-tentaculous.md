# Dr. Tentaculous (Mind Flayer)
*Monster Madness Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A psychological mastermind who disrupts opponents by manipulating their hands and discard piles. Dr. Tentaculous can use opponents' own discarded cards against them, representing a unique form of resource denial and mental control. His therapeutic approach to world domination makes him both terrifying and oddly professional.

## Playstyle
- **Mind Manipulation**: Control opponent psychology
- **Discard Pile Control**: Use opponent's discarded cards
- **Resource Denial**: Disrupt opponent strategies
- **Therapeutic Domination**: Professional psychological approach
- **Intellectual Superiority**: Outsmart rather than overpower

## Special Mechanics
### Discard Pile Manipulation
- **Card Recycling**: Use opponent's discarded cards
- **Mental Intrusion**: Access opponent's used resources
- **Strategic Denial**: Prevent opponent card recycling
- **Psychological Warfare**: Turn their own cards against them

### Therapeutic Theme
- **Professional Approach**: Clinical psychological methods
