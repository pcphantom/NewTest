# Blorp (Gelatinous Cube)
*Monster Madness Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A surprisingly powerful and aggressive monster whose signature ability allows him to "Hug" opponents, dealing damage that bypasses their Defense cards entirely. Combined with high overall damage output, this makes Blorp a top-tier character despite his seemingly innocent appearance.

## Playstyle
- **Hug Attacks**: Bypass defense with physical embrace
- **Aggressive Monster**: High damage output focus
- **Digestive Powers**: Consume and process opponents
- **Surprising Speed**: Faster than expected for a cube
- **Innocuous Threat**: Deceptively powerful abilities

---

## Special Mechanics
### Hug Ability
- **Defense Bypass**: Hug damage ignores Defense cards completely
- **Direct Damage**: Goes straight to opponent's Hit Points
- **Physical Contact**: Represents gelatinous cube engulfing attack
- **Surprise Factor**: Often underestimated by opponents
