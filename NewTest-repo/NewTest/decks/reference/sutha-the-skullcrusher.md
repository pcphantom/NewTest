# Sutha the Skullcrusher (Barbarian)
*Base Game Character*

## Character Strategy
A direct and aggressive brawler whose deck is filled with high-damage attacks and abilities that reflect barbarian rage. Sutha excels at self-healing tied to her most powerful assaults, making her a formidable front-line fighter who gets stronger as the battle intensifies.

## Playstyle
- **Aggressive Offense**: High-damage attacks to overwhelm opponents
- **Rage Mechanics**: Power increases with combat intensity  
- **Self-Sustaining**: Healing abilities tied to powerful attacks
- **Direct Combat**: Straightforward, no-nonsense approach
