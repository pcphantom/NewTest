# Azzan the Mystic (Wizard)
*Base Game Character*

## Character Strategy
A highly versatile and powerful spellcaster who excels at card draw and board control. Azzan wields devastating area-of-effect spells and possesses the unique ability to swap Hit Point totals, making him a constant threat. Widely considered one of the strongest characters in the game.

## Playstyle
- **Spell Mastery**: Powerful magical attacks and effects
- **Card Advantage**: Excellent card draw capabilities
- **Board Control**: Area-of-effect spells hit multiple targets
- **HP Manipulation**: Unique ability to swap health totals
- **Versatile Power**: Strong in all phases of the game
