# Lia the Radiant (Paladin)
*Base Game Character*

## Character Strategy
A resilient and defensive character focused on survival and righteousness. Lia possesses the most potent healing abilities in the game, allowing her to outlast opponents through divine protection and restoration. Her primary weakness is a relative lack of "Play Again" cards, which can limit her turn-to-turn speed.

## Playstyle
- **Divine Protection**: Strong defensive capabilities
- **Superior Healing**: Best healing abilities in the game
- **Righteous Fury**: Justice-themed attacks
- **Endurance Strategy**: Outlast opponents through staying power
- **Measured Pace**: Fewer combo opportunities but steady power
