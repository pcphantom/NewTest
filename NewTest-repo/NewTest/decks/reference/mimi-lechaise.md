# Mimi LeChaise (Mimic)
*Monster Madness Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A highly situational and unpredictable character whose entire strategy revolves around copying the abilities of other players. Mimi's effectiveness is completely dependent on the other characters in the game, making her a true wild card with the potential for either greatness or spectacular failure.

## Playstyle
- **Mimicry Master**: Copy other characters' abilities
- **Situational Power**: Effectiveness depends on opponents
- **Deceptive Appearance**: Everything looks innocent
- **Adaptive Strategy**: Change tactics based on available abilities
- **High Risk/Reward**: Potential for incredible power or complete failure

---

## Special Mechanics
### Mimic Abilities
- **Shape Copying**: Take on appearance and abilities of other objects
- **Ability Mimicry**: Copy opponent character abilities
- **Deceptive Nature**: Cards appear as innocent objects
- **Situational Dependence**: Power level varies drastically by game

### Disguise Theme
- **False Objects**: Everything appears to be harmless furniture/items
- **Trap Denial**: Claims of innocence despite obvious danger
- **Shapeshifting**: Constantly changing appearance

## Tactical Notes
- **Strengths**: Incredible adaptability, can copy best abilities from other characters
- **Weaknesses**: Completely dependent on opponents, may have no useful abilities
- **Best Against**: Powerful, ability-heavy character lineups
- **Vulnerable To**: Simple, straightforward strategies with few copyable abilities

## Recommended Strategy
1. **Assessment Phase**: Evaluate what abilities are available to copy
2. **Adaptation**: Focus on copying the most powerful available abilities
3. **Opportunistic Play**: Take advantage of situational openings
4. **Denial Tactics**: Use innocent appearance to avoid early targeting
