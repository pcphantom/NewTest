# Minsc and Boo (Fearless Ranger)
*Battle for Baldur's Gate Expansion*

## Character Strategy
An aggressive and disruptive hero duo, accompanied by Boo the "miniature giant space hamster." Minsc excels at creating powerful card combinations and can interfere with opponents' strategies by stealing cards from their hands, representing chaotic good heroism with unpredictable tactics.

## Playstyle
- **Heroic Disruption**: Interfere with opponent strategies
- **Card Theft**: Steal and use opponent's cards
- **Combo Potential**: Chain effects for powerful turns
- **Chaotic Good**: Unpredictable but heroic actions
- **Boo Synergy**: Special interactions with hamster companion

---

## Special Mechanics
### Stolen Cards
- **Acquisition**: Take cards from opponent hands/decks
- **Usage**: Play stolen cards as if they were your own
- **Return**: After playing, stolen cards go to **original owner's** discard pile
- **Elimination**: If Minsc & Boo eliminated, return all stolen cards to owners
