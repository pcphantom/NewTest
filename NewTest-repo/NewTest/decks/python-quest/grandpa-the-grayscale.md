# Grandpa the Grayscale (Wizard)
*Python's Quest for the Holy Kale Expansion*

## Character Strategy
A cranky old wizard who mastered magic back when everything was in black and white. Grandpa the Grayscale controls the battlefield through nostalgia-powered spells and "back in my day" lectures that literally bore opponents into submission. His mastery of outdated technology and monochrome magic makes him a versatile controller who can adapt to any situation - just don't ask him about color.

## Playstyle
- **Monochrome Master**: Spell versatility with grayscale themes
- **Board Control**: Area-of-effect spells and disruption
- **Nostalgia Manipulation**: Turn order and card advantage
- **Old-School Magic**: Classic D&D spell parodies
- **Lecture Power**: Opponent disruption through tedious stories

---

## Special Mechanics
### Grayscale Theme
- **Monochrome Magic**: Spells reference black and white displays
- **Outdated Technology**: CRT monitors, dot matrix printers, floppy disks
- **Generation Gap**: "Back in my day" abilities that confuse younger opponents
- **Wisdom of Age**: Draw and manipulation effects

### Controller Archetype
- **Hand Manipulation**: Force discards and card denial
- **Saving Throws**: DC-based attacks that opponents must resist
- **Area Effects**: Multi-target spells
- **Resource Denial**: Prevent opponent combos

## Tactical Notes

### Strengths
- **Excellent Hand Control**: Multiple discard effects
- **Turn Manipulation**: Can delay/disrupt opponent turns
- **Card Advantage**: Strong draw spells
- **Area Damage**: Grayscale Bomb hits multiple targets
- **Saving Throw Pressure**: Forces opponents to roll often

### Weaknesses
- **Lower Defense**: Only 3 defense cards total
- **Setup Dependent**: Needs hand advantage to control effectively
- **Vulnerable to Aggro**: Fast decks can pressure before control established
- **Medium Damage Output**: No single huge damage spike

### Best Against
- **Combo Decks**: Disrupts their setup
- **Slow Strategies**: Controls tempo effectively
- **Equipment-Heavy Builds**: Can force discards before Equipment played
- **Other Control Decks**: Out-values them with draw

### Vulnerable To
- **Fast Aggro**: May not stabilize in time
- **High HP Tanks**: Long games favor their durability
- **Direct Damage Immunity**: Some decks resist saving throws
- **Simple Strategies**: Hard to disrupt straightforward beatdown

## Recommended Strategy

### Early Game (Turns 1-3)
1. **Establish Card Advantage**: Use Memory Leak early
2. **Disrupt Setup**: Boring Story to deny opponent hand
3. **Defensive Posture**: Hold shields for critical moments

### Mid Game (Turns 4-7)
1. **Control Tempo**: Use Loading... Please Wait to skip opponent turns
2. **Damage Pressure**: 8-Bit Blast and Dot Matrix Missile
3. **Maintain Hand**: Keep disruption available
4. **Screen Burn-In**: Stack damage on priority targets

### Late Game (Turns 8+)
1. **Game Reset**: The Olden Days if behind
2. **Phosphor Burn**: Lock down leading opponent
3. **Area Damage**: Grayscale Bomb weakened opponents
4. **Screen Saver Loop**: Reusable defense for survival

## Deck Strategy Deep Dive

### Opening Hand Priority
**Ideal Starting Hand**:
1. Memory Leak or Back In My Day (card advantage)
2. Boring Story (disruption)
3. 8-Bit Blast or defense (survival)

**Mulligan Considerations**:
- Keep if you have draw or disruption
- Mulligan if all high-cost with no setup
- Keep The Olden Days only if also have early game

### Matchup Guide

**vs Aggro Decks (Oriax, Sutha)**:
- Priority: Survive early onslaught
- Keep: CRT Screen Flicker, Monochrome Shield
- Use: Loading... Please Wait to skip their turns
- Win Condition: Stabilize, then out-value

**vs Control Decks (Azzan, Delilah)**:
- Priority: Out-card advantage them
- Key: Memory Leak early and often
- Counter: The Olden Days ruins their setup
- Win Condition: Grind them out of resources

**vs Tank Decks (Sutha, Lia)**:
- Priority: Chip damage over time
- Use: Phosphor Burn for persistent damage
- Avoid: Early all-in aggression
- Win Condition: Long game favors your control

**vs Equipment Decks**:
- Priority: Discard before they setup
- Key: Boring Story timing
- Counter: Force discards pre-Equipment
- Win Condition: Win before they assemble

### Advanced Combos

**The Lecture Combo**:
1. Play Memory Leak (draw 3)
2. Play Boring Story (force discard)
3. Opponent now has no hand while you have cards
4. Use Monochrome Lecture to prevent their Play Again
*Total: Massive tempo swing*

**The Reset Combo**:
1. Play The Olden Days (everyone draws 3, you draw 5)
2. Immediately play Boring Story (they discard 2)
3. Net result: They have 1 card, you have 5+
*Total: Near-lockout*

**The Burn Combo**:
1. Play Phosphor Burn on opponent
2. They take 1 damage per card played
3. Play Loading... Please Wait
4. They're forced to skip turn but still take damage
*Total: Damage over time plus control*

**The Shield Loop**:
1. Play Screen Saver (4 shields)
2. When broken, returns to hand
3. Play again next turn
4. Effective immortality if you can maintain tempo
*Total: Reusable defense*

## Lore & Background

### Character Backstory

*"Grandpa the Grayscale wasn't always called 'Grandpa.' Once, he was simply known as 'The Programmer' - a wizard who mastered the ancient arts of FORTRAN, COBOL, and Assembly. He cast his first spell using punch cards and learned magic from actual printed manuals (not PDFs!).*

*When the world shifted from monochrome to color, Grandpa refused to update his display drivers. 'Color is a fad,' he insisted, maintaining his mastery of 256 shades of gray. While younger wizards embraced RGB, HDR, and even 4K, Grandpa's CRT monitor still flickered with green phosphor text, and his magic remained just as potent.*

*Now, in the 2D Multiverse, Grandpa wields the power of outdated technology and endless lectures. His opponents learn the hard way that you don't need modern graphics to dominate - just wisdom, experience, and an intimate knowledge of how things USED to work.*

*His ultimate goal? To teach these young pixel-whippersnappers some respect for their elders, one boring story at a time."*
